Sendoff ✈️ Launch week · Nov 1–8

Win a send for two.

Official rules · last updated October 18, 2026

Join Sendoff+ between November 1 and November 8, 2026 and you're in the draw.

One winner gets a Sendoff trip for themselves and a friend — up to $200 per person — picked from the site and booked by us. Winner drawn November 9.

Open Sendoff →

The prize

  • One send for two people: the winner and one friend of their choice.
  • Sendoff pays the flights (or gas) and the place to stay, up to $200 per person, $400 in total, booked through the same links the site uses. Anything above that, and food, fun and everything else on the trip, is on you.
  • Pick any trip on sendofftrips.com that fits. Travel within 90 days of the draw, dates agreed with us.
  • No cash alternative. The prize isn't transferable, but you choose who comes with you.

How to enter

  • Join Sendoff+ (monthly or yearly) at sendofftrips.com between 12:00 am on November 1 and 11:59 pm on November 8, 2026, US Eastern time. One entry per account.
  • Or enter free. No purchase is necessary. Email support@sendofftrips.com with your name, city and the subject "Giveaway" in the same window. One free entry per person. Free entries and paid entries have the same chance of winning.

Who can enter

  • Anyone 18 or over with a US mailing address. Void where prohibited by law.
  • Marc and anyone working on Sendoff, and their immediate families, can't enter.

How the winner is chosen

  • Random draw from all valid entries on November 9, 2026, done on camera on @sendoff.trips.
  • We email the winner the same day. If they don't reply within 5 days, we draw again.
  • The winner's first name and city may be announced on Instagram and on the site. We'll ask before using anything else.

The fine print

  • The approximate retail value of the prize is $400. The winner is responsible for any taxes.
  • Joining Sendoff+ gets you the membership whether or not you win; cancelling before the draw cancels your entry.
  • This promotion is run by Sendoff (sendofftrips.com). It is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram or Meta.
  • Questions: support@sendofftrips.com.
