Privacy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Here's what Sendoff (sendofftrips.com) collects about you, where it's kept, and how to get rid of it. We keep this short because there isn't much.

What we collect

  • Browsing: the city, budget, crew size and dates you pick. These stay in your browser unless you have an account.
  • The signup list: name, email and city, if you fill in the "get on the list" box.
  • Accounts: name, email, city, the trips you save or mark done, your membership plan, and the look you've chosen.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Stripe. Sendoff never sees or stores your card number. We keep Stripe's customer and subscription IDs so we know what plan you're on.
  • Forms: whatever you type into the Personal Sendoff, Big Send or community forms.
  • Analytics: which pages are used and how, through Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. Clarity may record how a page is scrolled and tapped; it masks text you type.

Where it goes

Sendoff runs on a few services, each holding only what it needs:

  • Netlify hosts the site and receives the signup list.
  • Supabase holds accounts and saved trips.
  • Stripe takes payments.
  • Resend sends our emails: sign-in links and the occasional update. Every update email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Travelpayouts and its partners (airlines, Booking.com, Kiwi, Aviasales and others) get told a booking link came from Sendoff when you click one. What you do on their sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone beyond the services above.

Email

If you're on the list or have an account, we'll email you when Sendoff opens in your city and, now and then, about new trips or deals. Unsubscribe from any email with one click, or reply "stop". Sign-in links are only sent when you ask for one.

Cookies and storage

Sendoff uses your browser's storage to remember your picks, whether you're signed in, and whether you've seen the welcome screen. The analytics tools above set their own cookies. Clearing your browser data clears all of it.

Your choices

  • See or fix your data: most of it is on your profile. For anything else, email us.
  • Delete your account: email support@sendofftrips.com from the account's address and we'll delete it, usually within a few days. Stripe keeps payment records as long as the law requires.
  • Leave the list: the unsubscribe link in any email, or just ask.

Age

Sendoff is for people 18 and over. If you think someone younger has given us their details, tell us and we'll remove them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on the site or by email.
